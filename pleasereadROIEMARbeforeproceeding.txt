GUI decisions
- Barangay-specific workspaces; admins access all
- All active roles can manage disasters in their workspace
- Households require at least one confirmed member
- Homeless household address uses barangay name
- One household head represents the family for aid
- Age and member categories entered separately
- Each donation receipt creates a separate stock batch
- Select a batch when multiple batches have stock
- Approvals required before stock changes
- Keep GUI as the View; backend logic stays in Models
