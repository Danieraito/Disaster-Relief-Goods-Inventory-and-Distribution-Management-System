IMPORTANT DOMAIN DECISIONS
1. Barangay workspaces:
   - Each barangay has its own workspace.
   - Barangay officials and encoders are assigned to a barangay.
   - Active users can access their assigned barangay; admins can access all.
   - Active users of any role may create/update disaster records in workspaces they can access.
2. Inventory batches:
   - Each logged incoming receipt is a separate batch.
   - Batch quantity and optional expiry date belong to that batch.
   - Existing opening stock is represented by an opening batch.
   - If several batches have stock, a distribution must explicitly select a batch.
   - Do not silently assume FIFO or expiry-first selection.
3. Households and members:
   - Beneficiary records represent households.
   - A household requires at least one confirmed family member at registration.
   - Individual members can be recorded, with one designated household head representing the household for aid.
   - For a homeless household, the barangay name is recorded as the address.
   - Age and senior/infant/PWD categories are separate values, entered according to human verification. Do not infer category from age.
4. Donation and distribution workflow:
   - Status transitions: DRAFT -> PENDING -> APPROVED or REJECTED.
   - Only an approval changes inventory.
   - Approval and stock updates should be atomic once SQL is integrated.
5. Reports and audits:
   - Their methods/behaviors are intentionally deferred until after SQL. Keep the comments noting this.
